# Diagnostic Results

These files contain compact results and checksums. Complete episode records,
checkpoints, and logs remain under the excluded
`outputs/act_comparability/` directory.

- `offline_diagnostics.json` records the deterministic checkpoint analyses.
- `peg_rate_input_diagnostic.json` records the paired fixed-phase rate
  intervention.
- `upright_seed0_pilot.json` records training, candidate selection, and paired
  nominal development results for the bounded upright seed-0 pilot.
- `peg_seed0_pilot.json` records the corresponding keyed-peg seed-0 evidence.
